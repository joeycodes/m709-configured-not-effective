### test: detection (a) stays silent while tier 2 to tier 0 is blocked, stays silent while only one of the two blocking layers is open, and raises an incident once both are open (ADR D21; scenario #1's detection)
### note: this is the rule's own synthetic trigger, made to show that it fires. It is not a run of scenario #1, which is pre-registered and run in M3.
### note: tier 2 to tier 0 is blocked twice: on the NVA (nftables, fwd-t2t0-drop, R5) and on the tier-0 NSG (DenyTier2ToTier0, priority 200, inbound). VNet flow logs record a connection at every NIC it crosses (R4 (e)); a row at the tier-2 NIC or the NVA's inbound side says Allowed even when the NVA then drops the packet. Only an Allowed row at the tier-0 NIC means the packet arrived, so the rule selects on that.

### note: query_period is three times query_frequency on purpose. TimeGenerated in NTANetAnalytics is when Traffic Analytics processed the flow, minutes after it happened. With a period equal to the frequency, a row that lands just before a run's cutoff but after that run has read the table falls outside the next run's window and is never seen, with no error.
