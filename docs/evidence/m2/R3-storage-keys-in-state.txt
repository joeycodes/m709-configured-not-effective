### result
### Keeping keys out of the repository does not keep them out of reach: Terraform fetches them on every run and stores them next to everything else in state. Disallowing shared key is what makes that harmless; turning it on, even for a 50-minute test, made the state a credential store. Any future decision to keep shared key on (ADR D21) has to count this, together with who can read state.
